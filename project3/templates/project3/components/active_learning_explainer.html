How Active Learning Works Here

In this system, Active Learning is used to assess expert competence rather than to discover missing dataset labels.

  • Training Phase: The baseline model (trained to 87.8% accuracy) evaluates unlabeled text examples and selects a limited budget (N) of queries. By querying the expert on these strategic examples, the system estimates their topic-specific competence profile.
  • Testing Phase: Active querying stops. The system passes the baseline model weights and the learned competence profile to a competence-aware deferral policy. At test time, the system compares its own confidence against the expert's learned competence to decide whether to predict internally or defer to the expert.
Design Justification / Alternative Approaches

While Lecture 6 outlines three active learning scenarios—Pool-Based Sampling, Stream-Based Sampling, and Membership Query Synthesis—we explicitly chose Pool-Based Sampling.

  • Membership Query Synthesis was rejected because generating synthetic TF-IDF text features yields unnatural, nonsensical text instances that human experts cannot evaluate effectively.
  • Pool-Based Sampling leverages our natural AG News text corpus, ensuring queries remain representative, realistic, and fully human-interpretable.
