Simulated Expert(s)

Configure up to two simulated experts to model different human-AI collaboration scenarios. You can define each expert's behavioral profile by choosing their type: Trivial (perfectly accurate in selected domains, but completely wrong in others) or Realistic (exhibiting baseline competence levels with natural randomness). Customize their specific domains of expertise and set an operational cost per query to evaluate how different deferral policies balance human reliability against query budgets.
