This table displays from which classes a model with a certain strategy has chosen to query an expert and how accurate an expert was.

With the Balanced uncertainty (Information-based) strategy, the query policy selects low-margin classifier examples while balancing across predicted classes. Low-margin classifier examples are instances where the model's prediction is highly ambiguous (meaning the difference in probability or distance between the top two most likely classes is very small). Balancing across predicted classes (according to the model's current beliefs) ensures that queries are evenly distributed among all label types, preventing the model from over-sampling from a single category. The queried labels are used to estimate where the expert is likely to help.
